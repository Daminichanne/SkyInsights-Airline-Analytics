Business takeaways

Insights from the full dataset
EXPERIENCEWi-Fi is a key improvement area

Inflight Wi-Fi has the lowest average service rating in this dataset. Improving connectivity may help address a visible customer pain point.

SEGMENTATIONTravel purpose matters

Business travellers show substantially higher satisfaction than personal travellers. Review the needs and expectations of leisure customers separately.

CABIN STRATEGYEconomy has room to improve

Business-class passengers report higher satisfaction than economy passengers. Prioritise high-impact touchpoints across the economy journey.
